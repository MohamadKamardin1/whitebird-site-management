# White Bird Zanzibar Site Management Platform

## Cover

**White Bird Zanzibar Site Management Platform**

*One trusted operational picture — from cleaner to leadership*

Prepared for White Bird Organizations

## Slide 1

# A growing operation needs one clear system

- White Bird manages people, sites, supplies, reports, and service quality across many locations.
- Every supervisor needs simple tools; leadership needs reliable proof, not late updates.
- The goal is practical: make daily work easier and management decisions faster.

## Slide 2

# Today’s problems are expensive in time and trust

- Paper sheets, phone calls, and separate files make it difficult to confirm what happened at each site.
- Missing attendance, late stock requests, and incomplete cleaning checks are found too late.
- Managers spend time chasing information instead of solving the problem.
- There is little single evidence trail for who did what, when, and why.

## Slide 3

# The proposed solution: one operational control centre

- A secure web platform connects people, sites, tasks, stock, reporting, and supervision.
- Each user sees only the work and information that belongs to their role.
- Kiswahili is the default language, with English available in the header.
- Every important action is time-stamped, permission-controlled, and auditable.

## Slide 4

# One platform, from field work to management action

- **People:** Cleaner onboarding, trainee management, attendance, assignments, shifts, remuneration support.
- **Site quality:** Daily cleanliness sheets, issues, inspections, evidence, and supervisor follow-up.
- **Stock:** Store hierarchy, products, balances, movements, requests, approvals, and cost visibility.
- **Leadership:** Dashboards, reports, schedules, GIS maps, alerts, and AI-supported summaries.

## Slide 5

# Every role has a clear responsibility

- **Cleaner / Site Supervisor:** Complete daily work, attendance, cleanliness records, stock requests, and site issues.
- **Zone Supervisor:** Verify site conditions and stock requests physically; manage several sites.
- **Assistant General Supervisor / General Supervisor:** Approve, monitor performance, and act on cross-zone risk.
- **HR / Store Manager:** Onboard staff, manage assignments and stores, prepare supplies, and maintain records.
- **System Administrator:** Controls users, sites, zones, settings, permissions, audit, and organization-wide configuration.

## Slide 6

# Daily service becomes a simple, auditable routine

- Site Supervisor opens the day’s table and records cleaning answers one row at a time.
- Attendance captures sign-in, sign-out, half-day, absence, and responsible records.
- Problems are raised immediately with site context; evidence moves upward to the right supervisor.
- Leadership sees summaries rather than raw paper forms.

## Slide 7

# Stock moves with control — not confusion

- Stores can be configured as **Super Store → Power Store → Site Store**.
- Every item has a unit, cost, opening quantity, balance, and movement history.
- Transfers record source, destination, quantity, responsible person, and value.
- Weekly analysis shows which items are used most, at which stores, and at what cost.

## Slide 8

# Stock requests follow a responsible approval chain

- Site Supervisor prepares an item-by-item request before the monthly deadline.
- Zone Supervisor physically verifies quantities and site need.
- Assistant General Supervisor approves the verified request.
- HR receives the approved packing and assembly queue; central inventory closes only after completion.

## Slide 9

# HR onboarding is flexible and protected

- HR can register one cleaner or import a prepared Excel workbook.
- Before onboarding, HR chooses **Active** or **Trainee** status and the assigned site.
- A Trainee automatically enters the training programme; an existing verified worker can start as Active.
- Personal ID and phone data are masked by default and revealed only through an authorized, audited HR action.

## Slide 10

# Better evidence creates better decisions

- **Real-time accountability:** ownership, timestamps, approvals, and historical records are retained.
- **Faster supervision:** role-based dashboards surface exceptions instead of forcing managers to search for them.
- **Better service quality:** recurring cleanliness and attendance evidence supports client confidence.
- **Cost control:** item usage and stock-value trends identify waste, shortages, and high-demand sites.

## Slide 11

# A practical 90-day rollout reduces risk

- **Days 1–15:** Confirm sites, zones, users, products, stores, and operating rules.
- **Days 16–45:** Train HR, Store Manager, System Administrator, and supervisors; pilot selected sites.
- **Days 46–75:** Extend to all sites, verify attendance, cleaning, stock, and reporting routines.
- **Days 76–90:** Review adoption, refine dashboards, and establish monthly management review.
