## Implementation Implications

The platform must preserve these source headings and role labels without rewording. Administrator-managed timetable assignments must be personal, site- and zone-scoped, date-effective, shift-aware, and auditable. Zone Supervisors must only see their own roster rows and assigned sites; Assistant General Supervisors must only see the sites/zones assigned to their own roster rows. Checklist submissions must retain the responsible user, role, site/area, scheduled shift, work date, source checklist type, and immutable submission snapshot.
